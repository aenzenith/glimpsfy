Reading order in multi-column layouts

By the Glimpsfy team · 4 minute read

A page with two columns is read from
top to bottom in the left column first,
and only then in the right column. An
engine that sorts every line by its
height alone mixes the two columns.

The layout step finds the gutter between
the columns and keeps each column whole.

Headings that span both columns come
before the columns, and a footer that
spans them comes after both of them.

Tables are different: their cells are short,
so a row is read from left to right and
the rows follow each other downwards.
